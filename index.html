About us

TravelHutt began with one simple idea: finding and booking a good place to stay should be easy, clear and honest for everyone.

We started with the stays people dream about, from the long sandy beaches of Inani and Laboni to the city views around Shugondha in Cox's Bazar. Every hotel on our platform is listed with its real location, a clear price per night and photos, so you know what you're getting before you book.

TravelHutt is built to take the stress out of travel planning. Pick your destination, choose your dates, add your guests and book in a few taps. Pay the way you prefer, with bKash, Nagad or card. When your booking is confirmed, you can download your e-ticket as a PDF. It includes a QR code that opens your live booking details, so check-in is quick and there is no confusion.

We also work with a growing network of travel agents. With their own referral codes, agents can bring their customers to TravelHutt, follow those bookings and download their tickets. Our admin team reviews and confirms each booking, so you always see its true status: pending, confirmed or cancelled.


Our promise is simple:

  • Transparent: clear prices, clear booking status, no surprises.
  • Easy: a fast booking flow that works the same on your phone and your computer.
  • Reliable: a confirmed e-ticket and real people behind every booking.
  • Local: made in Bangladesh, for travelers who love exploring their own country and beyond.

Whether you're planning a weekend by the sea or a family holiday, TravelHutt is here to help you find the right stay and make every trip a story worth telling.
